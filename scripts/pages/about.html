This is archview on examples/pizzeria, a small pizza shop with two planted problems. Click a box to go inside it, a module to read its code, a line to see the imports behind it.

Both problems are in the kitchen's stations:

  • A cycle. toppings asks the oven how hot it is, and the oven takes pizzas from toppings.
  • A broken rule. The stations' rules say boxing takes pizzas from the oven only, but boxing imports dough to patch a torn base.

On your own code:

uv tool install git+https://github.com/LiorHalfon/arch-viewer@latest
cd your-repo && archview .
